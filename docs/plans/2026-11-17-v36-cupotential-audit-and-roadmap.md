## 2. Physics & Validation Audit: Root Causes of Every `L40S` Section 7 Finding

Cross-referencing `cuPotential` math against Prolix's `L40S` physics audit ([`261111_math_and_physics_audit.md`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/docs/validation/261111_math_and_physics_audit.md#L344-L530)) uncovered **exact, concrete bugs in Prolix today** explaining every major discrepancy:

### 2.1 NPT `-1.56%` to `-1.81%` Equilibrium Density Bug (`scale_influence_function_isotropic`)
- **Observed Section 7.3c Gap**: `TIP3P` (`0.9679` vs `0.9857 g/cm³`, `-1.81%`), `1UBQ` (`1.0221` vs `1.0390 g/cm³`, `-1.63%`), `DHFR_PME` (`1.0061` vs `1.0220 g/cm³`, `-1.56%`).
- **Exact Root Cause**: Unlike `cuPotential` (`cupo_kspace_refresh_influence`), Prolix's [`scale_influence_function_isotropic`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/pme.py#L932-L945) (called at `pme.py:1099, 1126` and [`pallas_ragged_queue.py:1436`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/pallas_ragged_queue.py#L1436)) approximates isotropic box scaling $\mathbf{L} = \lambda \mathbf{L}_0$ ($V = \lambda^3 V_0$) at fixed $\alpha$ as `G_ref * (V_ref / V_cur)` $= G_0 \lambda^{-3}$. Because $\mathbf{m}(\lambda \mathbf{L}_0) = \lambda^{-1}\mathbf{m}_0$, the exact transformation is:
  $$G(\mathbf{n}; \lambda \mathbf{L}_0) = G(\mathbf{n}; \mathbf{L}_0) \cdot \lambda^{-1} \exp\!\left(-\frac{\pi^2 |\mathbf{m}_0|^2}{\alpha^2}\left(\lambda^{-2} - 1\right)\right)$$
- **Quantitative Verification**: On our exact `895`-water `TIP3P` box ($L_0 = 30\text{ Å}, 32^3, \alpha = 0.34\text{ Å}^{-1}$), exact molecular-COM differentiation gives $P_{\text{recip}}^{\text{exact}} = -66.72\text{ bar}$ vs `+286.10 bar` from `scale_influence_function_isotropic` — a spurious **`+352.82 bar`** outward MC barostat pressure bias that predicts `-1.8%` to `-2.1%` density loss via $\kappa_T \approx 6.0\times 10^{-5}\text{ bar}^{-1}$.
- **Clean P0 Fix**: Recompute `influence_function(grid_dims, box_cur, alpha, order)` directly on GPU (`64×64×33`, `< 4 µs`), or cache $A_0 = \pi^2 |\mathbf{m}_0|^2 / \alpha^2$ alongside $G_0$ and apply $\lambda^{-1}\exp(-A_0(\lambda^{-2}-1))$. This preserves exact `jax.vjp` `dE/dbox` and exports cleanly in StableHLO.

### 2.2 NVT `-4.88 K` (`1UBQ`) / `-5.22 K` (`DHFR_PME`) Solute–Water Split (`settle.py:3133-3136`)
- **Observed Section 7.3b Gap**: `1UBQ` (`sol=294.03 K` vs `wat=298.91 K`, `-4.88 K`) and `DHFR_PME` (`sol=292.98 K` vs `wat=298.20 K`, `-5.22 K`).
- **Exact Root Cause**: In [`_do_rattle`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/settle.py#L3133-L3136), Prolix executes an extra `L2`-orthogonal `_rattle_vel_cluster_vec3(..., ab2, n_iters=6)` velocity projection **immediately after `R2` (before `F` and `B2`)**, whereas rigid water (`_do_water`, [`settle.py:2892-2898`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/settle.py#L2892-L2898)) stops after `R2 + AM2` and projects velocities **only once** after `B2`. Because bond axes rotate from `ab1` to `ab2` during `A2`, projecting `p_fin` onto $T_{r(t+\Delta t)}\mathcal{M}$ before `B2` drains $\Delta K = -\frac{1}{2}\mu (\mathbf{v}_{\text{fin}}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{ab2})^2 < 0$ every single step on `HBonds`-constrained protein solute, compounded by `float32` iterative SHAKE rounding (`validate_release_physics_and_observables.py:1987, 2225`).
- **Clean P0 Fix**: Delete the redundant post-`R2` projection at `settle.py:3133-3136` and adopt `fltflt` `(hi, lo)` constraint accumulators (Section 3.1).

### 2.3 Tier 1 PME Reciprocal Delta, Switched LJ Tail Gap, Exception PBC & NVE Drift
- **PME Reciprocal (`-0.2114` to `-14.3846 kcal/mol`, `F_RMSE = 0.00309–0.00934 kcal/(mol·Å)`)**: Caused by Prolix's order-4 Euler B-spline ([`pme.py:515-523`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/pme.py#L515-L523)) vs OpenMM `PmeOrder=5`. Upgrading Prolix's static 1D denominator $|b(m)|^2$ to `cuPotential`'s **IK / Hockney–Eastwood RMS-averaged discrete deconvolution** $|\hat{\phi}_{\text{disc}}(k, G)| = \sqrt{\langle |\text{DFT}(w(t))|^2 \rangle_{t\in\{0,1/16,\dots,15/16\}}}$ (`include/cupo/math/spreading.h:47-58`) improves `4×4×4` force accuracy at **zero step-time cost** with unchanged `_spme_bwd` adjoint and full StableHLO portability.
- **Tail Correction (`+0.03557` to `+0.03849 kcal/mol` Constant Offset + Switched Tail Bug)**:
  - The constant `+0.035–0.038 kcal/mol` difference against OpenMM is purely algebraic: OpenMM (`NonbondedForceImpl.cpp:350, 371-374`) weights diagonal atom classes by $c_a(c_a+1)/2$ and divides by $N(N+1)/2$, whereas [`explicit_corrections.py:129-131`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/explicit_corrections.py#L129-L131) sums ordered pairs over $N^2$. Because ~2/3 of TIP3P atoms have $\epsilon_H = 0$, the product $\propto N \cdot N^{-1}$ stays constant across `2.3k -> 92.2k` atoms.
  - **Genuine Missing Physics in Prolix**: [`lj_dispersion_tail_energy`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/physics/explicit_corrections.py#L104-L132) integrates only $[r_c, \infty)$ when `lj_switch_width > 0`, omitting the quintic switch integral deficit over $[r_{\text{switch}}, r_c]$. `plugins/openmm/cpp/openmmapi/src/lj_tail.hpp:28-59` (`Apache-2.0`) gives the exact rational + cancellation-free power-series closed form (`float64`/StableHLO-clean).
- **Reciprocal/Physical Exception PBC Bug ([`context.py:884, 893-895, 954`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/src/prolix/openmm/context.py#L884-L954))**: Uses non-periodic `r_ex_raw` for PME reciprocal `erf(alpha*r)/r` cancellation and ignores `getExceptionsUsePeriodicBoundaryConditions()`, causing a `~135 kJ/mol` mismatch when bonded/NBFix exceptions span periodic image boundaries (`test_far_exceptions.py`). Fix: always use MIC `r_ex_mic` for PME reciprocal `erf(alpha*r)/r` subtraction when `has_pbc=True`, and obey `exceptions_use_pbc` for physical 1-4 replacements. Also guard RF with `inv_eps = 1.0 / rf_dielectric` (`test_reaction_field.py:24-29`) to prevent conductor-limit `NaN`.
- **NVE Drift (`+0.02103` / `-0.05767 kcal/(mol·ns·dof)`)**: Stemming from unshifted `erfc` cutoff jumps at $r_c = 9.0\text{ Å}$ + `float32` accumulation; long-term P3 research can implement $C^2$-compact Prolate splitting (`arXiv:2505.09727`) + `C2OSpline<3>` cleanly in pure JAX polynomials in $r^2$ (`dS/d(r^2)` eliminates `sqrt`).
- **`P=6` Dispersion `LJ-PME` (P2 Feature)**: `samples/ltoir-usage/ewald.py:65-219` (`Apache-2.0`) provides exact JAX formulas for $b_i = 2\sqrt{\epsilon_i}\sigma_i^3$, $K_6(k^2)$ ($K_6(0) > 0$), positive $+(\alpha^6/12)\sum b_i^2$ self-energy, and the degree-24 cancellation-free excluded-pair series ($y < 2$), fully compatible with $O(N\cdot K)$ `@jax.custom_vjp` (`σ, ε, r, box`) and `prolix.export` StableHLO.

## 3. GPU Kernels & Performance Blueprint

### 3.1 `fltflt` `(hi, lo)` Accumulators in Pallas/Triton Without LLVM FMA Contraction (P1)
- **Measured FP64 Penalty (`L40S`, [`261111_l40s_pallas_v35_release_ladder.json`](file:///home/marielle/projects/prolix/docs/benchmarks/261111_l40s_pallas_v35_release_ladder.json))**: Because `L40S` (`sm_89`) and `RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell` (`sm_120`) have 1:64 FP64:FP32 throughput, Prolix `precision="mixed"` (`float64` state/SETTLE/RATTLE) slows down **`1.33x–2.66x`** vs `single` (`1UAO 2k`: `0.1229 -> 0.2341 ms`, `1.90x`; `DHFR 24k`: `0.6635 -> 0.8807 ms`, `1.33x`; `1AKE 45k`: `0.4944 -> 1.1709 ms`, `2.37x`; `ApoA1 92k`: `1.4564 -> 3.8801 ms`, `2.66x`), whereas OpenMM `CUDA mixed` pays only `~1.07x` (`0.1557 -> 0.1664 ms` on `DHFR_24k`).
- **`cuPotential` Technique (`plugins/openmm/cpp/platforms/cuda/src/integrator/fltflt.hpp`, `BSD-3-Clause`)**: Uses native `double` only when probed `cudaDevAttrSingleToDoublePrecisionPerfRatio <= 2` (`t64/t32 <= 3.0`), and elsewhere uses ~48-bit FPAN `(hi, lo)` pair arithmetic (`Zhang & Aiken SC'25` 20-op branch-free `add`, 7-op `mul`, `MUFU.RCP`-seeded Karp `div`, `rsqrtf`+FMA `sqrt`), with `(pos_hi, pos_lo)` stored as separate planes so force kernels read `pos_hi` directly with **zero FP64 casts**.
- **Pallas/Triton Lowering Rule**: Plain `jnp` `TwoSum`/`TwoProd` inside `pl.pallas_call` is unsafe because LLVM/NVPTX contracts `mul + add` and simplifies `s - (s - a)`, collapsing error terms `e -> 0`. Instead, wrap the 4 IEEE round-to-nearest-even primitives using `jax.experimental.pallas.triton.elementwise_inline_asm` (`"add.rn.f32 $0, $1, $2;"`, `"sub.rn.f32 $0, $1, $2;"`, `"mul.rn.f32 $0, $1, $2;"`, `"fma.rn.f32 $0, $1, $2, $3;"` without `.ftz`, `is_pure=True`), falling back to `float64` `jnp` under `prolix.export.in_export_context()` or `interpret=True`.
